# Biome enemies

Fact: The underground biomes use the Industrial_33_Enemies_Full_Animation_Pack roster: Maintenance 8, Factory 8, Boiler 9, and Toxin Refinery 8. Regular waves and boss reinforcements stay within their biome's roster. Existing bosses remain separate.

Why: The user requested the supplied enemies for each biome.

How to apply: Preserve assignments in data/enemies/industrial.json when regenerating room catalogs. tools/export_industrial_enemies.cjs exports transparent directional animation atlases from the pack's procedural models; tools/integrate_four_biomes.py assigns the rosters to rooms. All regular enemies now have three playable moves in data/enemies/abilities.json, generated by tools/build_industrial_abilities.py. Keep warning, active attack, and recovery animation timing aligned with those moves. Summons must count toward room clearance, remain biome-specific, and obey owner/room caps; player debuffs must expire and clear between rooms. See docs/industrial-enemies.md for prototype adaptations and validation. Elite variants remain separate.
