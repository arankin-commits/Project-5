# Industrial enemy integration

The 33 supplied designs populate regular waves in their source biomes: Maintenance (8), Factory (8), Boiler (9), and Toxin Refinery (8). Reinforcements also use the current biome. Existing bosses are retained.

The pack contains procedural prototype models rather than ready-to-use transparent game sprites. `tools/export_industrial_enemies.cjs` renders those models into 33 transparent SVG atlases under `art/industrial_enemies/` and writes `data/enemies/industrial.json`. Each atlas contains eight directions and sixteen poses covering idle, walking, windup, attack, recovery, and death. Original gallery images and model sources are preserved.

`scripts/industrial_enemy.gd` extends existing enemy combat. It uses supplied health and movement speed (26 game pixels per source unit, with existing difficulty scaling). `data/enemies/abilities.json` defines three playable moves per enemy, 99 total. `tools/build_industrial_abilities.py` explicitly maps each named move to its gameplay parameters and copies source windup, recovery, and cooldown durations. Ready moves cycle in source order, skipping unavailable cooldowns and hook-dependent moves without a caught target.

Attacks include directional melee, delayed combos, shots and radial volleys, marked lobs, expanding rings, timed damage zones, lunges, one-bounce rockets, bent rushes, sidesteps, and retreat moves. Shields reduce frontal directional damage by 85%; ground hazards and radial core damage bypass the guard. Hooks enable collision-aware pulls, with dodge and striking the source breaking attachments. Teleports choose nearby visible, connected floor. Foreman summons count toward room completion, with six live summons per owner and an eighteen-enemy room ceiling. Link Crawlers split into two independently targetable bodies with divided health; summons cannot recursively summon or split.

Bellows buffs a nearby melee ally's damage by 25% for four seconds. Player statuses implement 40% reduced energy gains, 25% reduced attack damage, energy theft, poison, clinging damage, breakable health siphoning, and local haze. Reapplying a status refreshes its duration instead of stacking it. Statuses and temporary attacks clear on chamber completion/transition. `scripts/industrial_attack.gd` owns attack collision, timing, and effects under the existing Projectiles container.

Animation poses now follow windup, active attack, recovery, movement, and death states; windups progress with their authored duration. The source provides shared pose sets rather than a separate animation clip for each named move. Distinct floor warnings, cables, guard arcs, projectiles, damage zones, phantom motion, and buff tint communicate the moves.

Adaptations: this is a 2D prototype implementation, not a literal reproduction of every prose detail. Swarm groups generally use one actor/hitbox and the source's composite sprite; Link Crawlers split into two such actors. Flying enemies retain ground navigation/collision. Vent Transit uses a marked safe floor destination rather than requiring authored vent nodes. Crane Hooker favors nearby existing ground hazards when available, otherwise reels toward itself. There is no passive energy regeneration in the game, so interference reduces hit-generated energy. Elite variants and new boss moves are outside this regular-enemy implementation. Combat balance still needs human playtesting.

Deaths disable collision immediately, emit defeat once, and retain the animation briefly before removing the corpse. This lets existing wave and reward progression continue without counting a corpse as a living enemy.

Regenerate with `node tools/export_industrial_enemies.cjs`, `python tools/build_industrial_abilities.py`, then `python tools/integrate_four_biomes.py`, and import with Godot. The supplied statistics CSV import sidecars use Keep File so Godot does not interpret statistics as localization tables.

Validation: `tests/test_industrial_enemies.gd` checks roster coverage, stats, atlases, directions, poses, and death cleanup. `tests/test_industrial_abilities.gd` runs every move through windup, release, and recovery and checks actual melee damage, shield direction, hazard bypass, status effects, hooks, wall collision, ricochet, lob delay, poison ticks, buffs, summon caps, and cleanup. `tests/capture_industrial_abilities.gd` renders warnings and active effects. `tests/test_industrial_live.gd` runs real physics and scripted dodge/attack inputs in a rendered chamber; its increased player health makes this a smoke test, not a balance test. `tests/test_underground_route.gd` checks all 36 rooms and boss transitions. The older skill test's retired-layout wall coordinate was replaced with an explicit wall fixture.
