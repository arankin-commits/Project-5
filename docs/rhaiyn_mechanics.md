# Rhaiyn Newby - Project 5 Mechanics

## Grapple
Input: Q
The player grapples to a point within 12 meters at 14 meters per second. Grapple has a 4-second cooldown. After the grapple ends, the player gains Momentum for 1.5 seconds.

## Ground Slam
Input: E while airborne
The player slams toward the ground and deals 30 damage within a 3-meter radius. Ground Slam has a 5-second cooldown. During Momentum, it deals 50 damage within a 5-meter radius.
